### 🚀 What I'm Working On

- 🚁 **UAV Research & Development:** Actively researching and writing code for the **PX4** and **ROS2** ecosystems, focusing on autonomous flight simulations and complex geofence mapping architecture.
- 🛠️ **Drone Engineering:** Applying software and simulation research toward the physical construction and programming of a custom drone. A major long-term goal is designing open-source PX4 development kits utilizing European-sourced single-board computers, primarily the **Raspberry Pi**, to maximize accessibility for academic research.
- 🎓 **Academic Progression:** Preparing for a Master's degree in Mechanical and Electrical Engineering to advance expertise in applied robotics.
